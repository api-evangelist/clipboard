# Clipboard Health

> Clipboard Health (also operating as "Clipboard" / Clipboard Works) is a Y Combinator (W17) and Andreessen Horowitz backed healthcare workforce marketplace connecting healthcare facilities with credentialed professionals to fill open shifts. It serves long-term care, home health and hospice, hospitals, and dental practices across the United States, with 6,500+ workplaces and ~1M workers. Clipboard Health is a product/marketplace company: it publishes no public product API, OpenAPI, or developer portal — this llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from public sources.
